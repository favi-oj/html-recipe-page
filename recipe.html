How to Prepare Nigerian Jollof Rice

Nigerian Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish prepared with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and spices. It is commonly served at family gatherings, celebrations, and special occasions. This recipe explains how to prepare delicious Nigerian Jollof Rice using simple ingredients and cooking methods.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of long-grain rice
  • 5 fresh tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 medium-sized onions
  • 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 2 cups of chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly with clean water and drain it.
  2. Wash the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, and onions.
  3. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and one onion until smooth.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil in a large cooking pot over medium heat.
  5. Chop the remaining onion and fry it in the hot oil until softened.
  6. Add the tomato paste and fry for about 2 minutes while stirring.
  7. Pour the blended tomato mixture into the pot and cook for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Add the seasoning cubes, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, and salt.
  9. Pour in the chicken or vegetable stock and bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  10. Add the washed rice and stir gently until it is evenly coated with sauce.
  11. Add a little water if necessary, cover the pot, and reduce the heat.
  12. Allow the rice to simmer for about 25 to 35 minutes until fully cooked. Add small amounts of water if needed.
  13. Turn off the heat and allow the rice to rest for 5 minutes.
  14. Fluff the rice gently with a fork and serve warm.

Important Safety Notes

Warning: Be careful when handling hot cooking oil to prevent burns and injuries.

Food Safety: Always wash your hands, cooking utensils, and vegetables before preparing the meal.

Hot Steam: Open the cooking pot carefully to avoid burns from escaping steam.

Important: Cook the rice thoroughly and ensure it is safe to eat before serving.

Remember: Refrigerate leftover rice promptly, preferably within one hour, and reheat thoroughly before eating.

Cooking Terms and Definitions

Simmering
Cooking food gently in liquid at a temperature just below boiling. This helps the Jollof Rice cook evenly.
Blending
Combining ingredients using a blender to create a smooth mixture, such as blended tomatoes, peppers, and onions.
Sauteing
Cooking ingredients quickly in a small amount of oil over medium or high heat. This method is used to soften onions and develop flavour.
Seasoning
Adding salt, herbs, spices, or other flavouring ingredients to improve the taste of food.
Fluffing
Gently separating cooked rice grains with a fork to prevent clumping and improve the texture before serving.
